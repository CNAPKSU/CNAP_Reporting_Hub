What the funding labels mean

Each opportunity carries one of three milestone labels. The labels show how an award relates to the R01 milestone: independent funding through an R01 or an equivalent award, which every CNAP project leader works toward.

Counts toward the R01 milestone
The award is an R01 or an equivalent award. Winning it meets the milestone.
Bridge funding
A smaller or shorter award that keeps your research going and builds preliminary data on the way to an R01-equivalent award. Bridge funding doesn’t meet the milestone by itself, but it can strengthen your next application.
Ask your program officer
Whether the award counts depends on its terms. Ask your program officer and the CNAP team before you apply.

NIH opportunities for IDeA states

Beyond the Center’s own award, the National Institute of General Medical Sciences (NIGMS) offers co-funding, instrumentation grants, training grants, and the Support for Research Excellence program to Institutional Development Award (IDeA) states such as Kansas.

Opportunity numbers and dates change every cycle. Confirm each one in the NIH Guide and the Notice of Funding Opportunity (NOFO) Forecast before you plan around it.

For an unfunded R01, R15, or R35

IDeA research project grant co-funding

NIGMS co-funds strong applications that align with NIH priorities but were not selected for funding, when the investigators and performance sites are in IDeA-eligible states.

  • Covers R01, R15, and NIGMS Maximizing Investigators’ Research Award (MIRA) R35 applications.
  • Applications with multiple principal investigators are not eligible.
  • Considers applications from the three prior council rounds, starting in May. For fiscal year 2026, those were May 2025, October 2025, and January 2026.
  • The institute that reviewed the application makes the nomination. NIGMS pays up to $350,000 in total costs for the first two years.

If your application scored well but was not funded, talk with the program officer assigned to it.

Support for Research Excellence (SuRE)

For faculty research at institutions that award science degrees and receive limited NIH support.

  • SuRE R16 (PAR-24-144), for faculty not currently funded by an NIH research project grant.
  • SuRE-First R16 (PAR-24-145), for investigators who have never held an independent external research grant.
  • SuRE Resource Center U24 (PAR-21-227), for capacity building, outreach, and training.

Administrative supplements

Supplements to an existing award now go through PA-26-001. NIGMS announces its areas of interest as Highlighted Topics, not as notices of special interest.

NIGMS is releasing fewer, simpler NOFOs, and release can be slower than in past years. NIGMS cannot discuss dates for unpublished opportunities, so watch the NIH Guide and the NOFO Forecast.
