CNAP pilot project

Investigating neuronal communication with transcranial magnetic stimulation

CNAP Pilot Project, 2024 request for applications

This project uses a new approach to measure conduction delays in the human brain non-invasively. The results will provide new neurophysiological evidence of how areas of the human brain communicate, which matters for both basic and clinical neuroscience and can open new opportunities for developing neuromodulation approaches.

About

Assistant Professor, Psychological Sciences, and director of the Brain Stimulation and Neuroplasticity Laboratory. Ph.D., D.Sc. His research looks at how connections in the human brain work and develops new ways to bring about neuroplasticity, joining basic and clinical research, with a focus on new therapies after stroke and for Parkinson's disease.
