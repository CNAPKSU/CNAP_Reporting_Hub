CNAP research project

Repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation to reduce hyperexcitability in Fmr1 knockout rats

Mentor: Ragozzino, University of Illinois Chicago

This project examines how low-frequency repetitive transcranial magnetic stimulation decreases cortical hyperexcitability in the frontal cortex of Fmr1 knockout rats, a model of Fragile X syndrome, during cognitive flexibility. It is important to understand how altered neural function and hyperexcitability affect cognition. Decreasing cortical hyperexcitability can restore normal physiology and support translational comparisons to human neural data.
