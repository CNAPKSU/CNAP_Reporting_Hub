CNAP pilot project

Mitigating Neural Hypoexcitability in Women Undergoing Disuse

January 26, 2026 to January 25, 2027 · Mentor: Benjamin Miller, Oklahoma Medical Research Foundation

This project aims to uncover the neural mechanisms driving disuse-induced muscle weakness and atrophy in midlife women and develop innovative treatment strategies for this at-risk population. Through a two-phase clinical trial, the team will investigate the adaptations within the motor cortex during wrist immobilization and rehabilitation. The findings will inform evidence-based, personalized prevention and rehabilitation programs to enhance patient outcomes.
