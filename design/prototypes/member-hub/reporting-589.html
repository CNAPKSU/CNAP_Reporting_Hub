What this report asks for

Each year, the Center collects the Current and Pending (Other) Support of each Core Director, Research Project Leader, and Pilot Project Leader for its Research Performance Progress Report (RPPR) to NIH.
This report has one effort question and one upload: your certified Current and Pending (Other) Support from SciENcv.

Planned change in effort

Will your CNAP effort drop by 25% or more in the next budget year, starting June 1?

NIH treats a Yes as a request for prior approval.
If the drop has already happened, please tell the evaluator, because a separate request is needed.

Read NIH’s exact wording (RPPR question D.2.a)
Will there be, in the next budget period, either (1) a reduction of 25% or more in the level of effort from what was approved by the agency for the PD/PI(s) or other senior/key personnel designated in the Notice of Award, or (2) a reduction in the level of effort below the minimum amount of effort required by the Notice of Award?

Please follow these three steps:

Current and Pending (Other) Support

  • Build your Current and Pending (Other) Support in SciENcv with NIH’s Common Form.
  • Certify your Current and Pending (Other) Support in SciENcv, then download the certified PDF.
  • Upload the certified PDF below.

Don’t flatten the PDF

NIH checks the SciENcv certification, and flattening removes the certification.

Your ORCID iD

Your ORCID iD must show in the Persistent Identifier (PID) section.

Malign Foreign Talent Recruitment Program (MFTRP) certification

Every year, NIH requires each senior/key person to certify in the Research Performance Progress Report (RPPR) whether the senior/key person is a party to a Malign Foreign Talent Recruitment Program (MFTRP).

Follow these four steps:

  1. Download your form below. Your name is already filled in.
  2. Sign the form with an Adobe digital signature. The guide below walks you through the signature.
  3. Flatten the signed form, as NIH asks. Print the form to PDF (Microsoft Print to PDF on Windows, or Save as PDF on a Mac).
  4. Upload the flattened PDF here.
  • Download your certification form (PDF, your name already filled in)
  • How to sign the certification with an Adobe digital signature (step-by-step guide, PDF)
  • Or the blank form (Word)
  • NIH’s notice on the MFTRP certification, NOT-OD-26-018 (opens in a new tab)
Signed MFTRP certification (flattened PDF)
