Cognitive Neuroscience (CN) Core User Survey

Not started

This survey asks how well the Cognitive Neuroscience Core's spaces, equipment, and support worked for you, and how often you used the Cognitive Neuroscience Core.
The Cognitive Neuroscience Core directors receive a report of combined answers and written comments, without names.

  • Your answers cover iDescribe everything since the last reporting session closed on May 31, 2026, through November 30, 2026. For example, a talk given on June 1 counts here, and a talk given on May 31 belongs to the last reporting session. If you left something out of an earlier reporting session, you can report it now. The surveys and reports open on October 15 and are due on November 30.June 1 to November 30, 2026
  • Due iSubmit each survey and report by 5:00 PM Central Time on Monday, November 30. Until then, a reminder email comes every 4 days, listing only what is left. After the due date, the surveys and reports stay open until the evaluator closes the reporting session.Monday, November 30, 2026

Other respondents never see your answers.
