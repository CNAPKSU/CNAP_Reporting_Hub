What to upload

Upload a 1–2 page Word document covering your accomplishments, your plans for the next period, and any changes.
Suggested file name convention example: Cain_Accomplishments.

What goes in your documentYour document should contain the following:

Accomplishments

  • Activities
  • Objectives
  • Significant Results
  • Key Outcomes
  • Unmet Goals
  • What experiments you completed (give reference to aims/milestones/timeline)
  • What you found
  • Dissemination of information

Plans

Provide a summary of what you plan to do during the next reporting period to accomplish your project goals and objectives.
Discuss efforts to ensure that the approach continues to be scientifically rigorous and results are robust and unbiased.

Remember that significant changes in objectives and scope require prior approval of the agency (e.g., NIH Grants Policy Statement, 8.1.2.).
Include any important modifications to the original plans.
Provide a scientific justification for any changes involving research with human subjects or vertebrate animals.
A detailed description of such changes must be provided under Changes.
Also discuss specific plans for any grant submissions stemming from the pilot grant.

Changes

Describe challenges or delays encountered during the reporting period (June 1 to November 30, 2026) and actions or plans to resolve those challenges.
Describe only significant challenges that may impede the research (e.g., accrual of patients, hiring of personnel, need for resources or research tools) and emphasize their resolution.
