Equipment and technology

Primary EEG system

Electrical Geodesics, Inc. (EGI) EEG systems

Six 64-channel Geodesic Sensor Nets (GSN) with full-head coverage across the scalp, face, and neck. Hydrocel nets allow rapid setup, about 15 minutes, supporting efficient experimental throughput.

Amplifiers
One Net Amps 400, the main amplifier, and one Net Amps 300
Software
Net Station 5 for acquisition and analysis
Data
MFF format, exported to EDF+, MATLAB, EEGLAB, FieldTrip, or custom pipelines

Mobile and wireless EEG

OpenBCI systems

Portable systems with small, medium, and large EEG caps and Cyton and Daisy biosensing boards in a 16-channel configuration. They record EEG and EMG and transmit wirelessly.

Transmission
Radio frequency or Bluetooth Low Energy
Sampling
125 hertz (Hz) per channel
Software
MATLAB, EEGLAB, FieldTrip, ERPLAB, OpenViBE, Lab Streaming Layer (LSL), BrainBay, and BioEra

Phase 2

Transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) and repetitive TMS (rTMS)

Integrated with EEG, these systems will enable causal manipulation of neural activity, so researchers can directly test the role of specific brain regions in cognitive processes and strengthen translational neuroscience applications.

Services

  • Study design consultation and methodological guidance
  • Experimental programming, setup, and troubleshooting
  • EEG data collection assistance and session support
  • Pre-collection validation: IRB approval verification, timing tests, and system checks
  • Coordination of equipment readiness and technical troubleshooting
  • Future support for TMS and rTMS experimental design and safety workflows

Custom scripting and data analysis support are offered through the Neuroinformatics Core.

CONNECT Lab

A separate facility, with support from two cores

Cog + Neuro Network for Experimentation and Computing Technologies. A staffed drop-in room, BH 444, with four workstations for R, MATLAB, Python, JMP, PsychoPy, Unity, and more.

Experiment assistanceThrough the Cognitive Neuroscience Core: experiment programming and design consultation, hardware integration with EEG, TMS, eye tracking, and response devices, help adapting existing scripts, and shared experiment code and templates.
Data assistanceThrough the Neuroinformatics Core: data pipelines and analysis consultation, workflows for large-scale and parallel analysis on Beocat, machine learning help, and workshops on analysis tools and methods.

Impact

  • Enables advanced human cognitive neuroscience research at scale
  • Provides centralized EEG and neuroimaging infrastructure for CNAP investigators
  • Strengthens methodological rigor and reproducibility in cognitive neuroscience studies
  • Expands experimental capabilities by integrating EEG with brain stimulation
  • Strengthens competitiveness for extramural funding in cognitive and translational neuroscience
