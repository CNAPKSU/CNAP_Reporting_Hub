Your first month

Six conversations that make the rest of your award easier, best held in your first month.

  1. 1

    Your onboarding meeting

    The CNAP team schedules this meeting for you, your mentor, and the Center Director. Together you go over your project, your milestones, and how your award year runs.

    Read the mentee guide to prepare

  2. 2

    Your mentor, Dr. Elena Voss

    Set your regular meetings and agree on how often you will meet. Please bring your aims page and your timeline.

    About your mentoring agreement

  3. 3

    Heather Bailey, Director

    After your onboarding meeting, reach out to the Director any time for introductions to people and resources across the Center.

    Email Heatherhbailey@ksu.edu

  4. 4

    Morgan Wills, Assistant Director for Grants and Contracts

    Plan your path to the R01 milestone, from your first submission to resubmission.

    Book a timemkwills@ksu.edu

  5. 5

    Roxana Ortiz, Grant Specialist and Accountant

    Set up your budget, purchasing, and travel reimbursements before your first expense.

    Email Roxanaroxana@ksu.edu

  6. 6

    The research cores you will use

    Meet with the staff of each core you will use before you book time: a tour of the Behavioral Neuroscience Core, or a consultation with the Cognitive Neuroscience or Neuroinformatics Core. The core directors walk you through the equipment, the training you need, and how to schedule.

    See the research cores
