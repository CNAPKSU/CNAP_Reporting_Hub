Your first month

Five conversations that make the rest of your award easier. Most new project leaders have all five in their first four weeks.

  1. 1

    Your mentor, Dr. Elena Voss

    Set your first meeting and agree on how often you will meet. Bring your aims page and your timeline.

    Read the mentee guide

  2. 2

    Heather Bailey, Director

    Introduce yourself and your project. The Director knows every project and can point you to the right people.

    Email Heatherhbailey@ksu.edu

  3. 3

    Morgan Wills, Assistant Director for Grants and Contracts

    Plan your path to the R01 milestone, from your first submission to resubmission.

    Book a timemkwills@ksu.edu

  4. 4

    Roxana Ortiz, Grant Specialist and Accountant

    Set up your budget, purchasing, and travel reimbursements before your first expense.

    Email Roxanaroxana@ksu.edu

  5. 5

    The research cores you will use

    Take an orientation with each core before you book time. Each core page lists its services and how to start.

    See the research cores
