The CNAP weekly speaker series

NeuroSync

Every Wednesday, a researcher from K-State or beyond brings new work to the whole Center, followed by questions and conversation over refreshments.

When
Wednesdays, 2:30 to 3:30 p.m.
Where
Leadership Room 127
Who comes
CNAP members are expected to attend. Guests are welcome.

This week at NeuroSync

PRHeadshot to come

Wednesday, November 18
2:30 to 3:30 p.m., Leadership Room 127

Dr. Priya Raman

Associate Professor, Psychological Sciences, Kansas State University

Memory consolidation during sleep

How slow waves and sleep spindles decide which memories last, in younger and older adults. Dr. Raman will share new recordings from her lab’s overnight studies, and what they suggest about memory in aging.

About Dr. Raman

Dr. Raman studies how sleep shapes memory across the lifespan, using overnight electroencephalography (EEG) and behavioral testing. She leads a CNAP research project and collaborates with the Cognitive Neuroscience Core.

Invited speaker · Wednesday, December 2

DKHeadshot to come

The talk

Machine learning for spike sorting

Dr. Daniel Kim

Associate Professor of Computer Science and director of the Neural Data Lab, Iowa State University

About the speaker

Dr. Kim builds machine-learning tools that sort the electrical signals of single neurons from large recordings, so that labs can study thousands of cells at once. His lab shares its methods as open-source software used by groups across the country.

Recognition

  • Early Career Award, a national neuroscience society, 2024
  • Funded by the National Institutes of Health and the National Science Foundation
