Housing rats on a reversed light cycle: tips for a new lab

OM

Omar MendesGraduate student, Biology · Started November 5 · 2 replies

Our room switches to a reversed light cycle in January. What should I know about lighting, daily checks, and handling during the dark phase?

2 replies

  1. TB

    Tom BrandtBehavioral Neuroscience CoreLast week

    Use red lights for checks during the dark phase, and handle the rats at the same time each day. Give them two weeks to adjust before testing.

    Ask for an introduction

  2. SR

    Dr. Samuel ReyesMentorLast week
