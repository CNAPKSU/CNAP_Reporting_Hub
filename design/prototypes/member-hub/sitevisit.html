Why it matters

The Site Visit gives Center members an important opportunity to reflect on the past year and look ahead to the next. It also gives the Center’s External Advisory Committee a firsthand view of the Center’s progress and where it stands today. We hope everyone leaves motivated and encouraged in their work and role within the CNAP scientific community.

01

Stronger together

Mentors, project leaders, core staff, and students meet in person, and the conversations started here often become collaborations.

02

Science up close

Hear where each project and core stands, walk through the labs, and see the work of two dozen students and trainees on the poster walls.

03

The next generation

Questions

I’m not presenting. Do I need to prepare?

No. Reading the program, the map, and these questions is enough. The Center looks forward to the perspectives you bring to each discussion.

Where do I park?

Out-of-town guests park free at the Bluemont Hotel, and every venue is within walking distance. In-town guests can buy a $6 daily permit for the Union Parking Garage through the parking app, since street parking isn’t allowed before 9:00 a.m. Parking on campus is free after 5:00 p.m., so the banquet is easy.

Can I get a ride?

Uber and other rideshares run in Manhattan, but cars can be scarce. Book ahead and allow extra time, or arrange a ride with your CNAP mentee.

What will the weather be like?

Late May is usually mild: highs in the mid-70s to low 80s, evenings in the upper 50s to low 60s, and a chance of an afternoon thunderstorm. Rooms can be cool, so bring layers, a light jacket, and an umbrella.

Is there Wi-Fi?

Yes. Guests from outside K-State can join the “KSU Guest” network.

Can I join online?

Yes. Every session, including the keynote, has a Zoom option. The link comes in your confirmation email.

What about meals?

Breakfast is at the hotel and at the Business Building each morning. Tuesday lunch is a boxed lunch for everyone who RSVPs, labeled for dietary needs. Wednesday lunch is informal: plenty of colleagues head out together after the poster session.

How are photographs used?

Group photographs may appear on the CNAP website, in presentations, and in Center materials. You chose your photography preference when you RSVPed, and Morgan Wills can change it for you.

How do I get reimbursed for travel?

Keep your receipts and email them to Roxana Ortiz after the visit. Lodging, hotel parking, and breakfast are already covered, so you won’t be charged for those.
