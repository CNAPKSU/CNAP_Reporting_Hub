What I wish I had known before my first R01 submission

DO

Dr. David OseiPilot project alumnus · Started October 25 · 5 replies

Five things I would do differently before my first R01: start the aims page earlier, ask for letters sooner, budget time for the biosketch, run the timeline past a program officer, and have a colleague outside my field read the significance section.

Please add your own. This thread is meant for anyone planning a first submission.

Your R21 is due in 123 days. This thread was matched to you.
